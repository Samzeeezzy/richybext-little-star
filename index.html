05 — Contest Rules

Fair play. Bright futures.

Official rules can be updated by the organisers before the contest opens.

01

Contestants must meet the age and eligibility requirements set by the organisers.

02

A parent or legal guardian must complete registration and provide required consent.

03

Contestant photographs and information must be submitted with appropriate permission.

04

Voting must follow the published voting rules. Attempts to manipulate votes may lead to disqualification.

05

Final winners will be determined according to the official competition rules and verified results.
